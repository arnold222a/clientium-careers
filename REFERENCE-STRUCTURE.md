# Reference structure: consulting.com/careers

Fetched 2026-09-28 (https://consulting.com/careers, no tracking params) plus two role
detail pages (/careers/short-form-editor, /careers/video-editor), because the index page
is only a list of roles and the real "recruiting page" pattern lives on each role page.
Only STRUCTURE and flow were taken. No copy, logo or images reused.

## Index page (/careers)
1. Top nav (Home, products, About Us, Careers) with a persistent **"Apply Now"** button top right.
2. **"Open Roles"** list: each row = department tag + role title + location (Remote) + type (Full-Time), whole row links to the role page.
3. Footer: disclaimer, Privacy Policy, Terms.

## Role detail page (the pattern we mirror)
1. **Nav + sticky "Apply Now"** CTA always visible.
2. **Breadcrumb**: Careers > Role name.
3. **Hero**: big role title + meta chips (Department / Location / Type).
4. **Intro / positioning** (2 short paragraphs): what the role is, then a reframe line
   ("you are not just editing clips, you are ...") that sets the ambition.
5. **What You'll Do**: 6-8 bullet responsibilities, output-oriented, mention high daily volume.
6. **AI in Your Timeline** (video editor page): dedicated block on how AI tools are part of the workflow.
7. **The Ideal Candidate**: 6-7 trait bullets (mindset, not skills).
8. **Requirements**: hard requirements bullets (formats, daily output, deadlines, pipelines).
9. **Software Skills**: tools listed with proficiency level (Proficient / Basic knowledge).
10. **About the company**: one short paragraph (who they are, how they operate).
11. **Culture proof** ("Company Challenges"): cards of team experiences (city + year + activity).
12. **Coaching and Development**: how you grow, feedback loops, more ownership for strong performers.
13. **"We only work with A-players"**: standards statement.
14. **"You Shouldn't Apply If"**: 3 honest disqualifiers (not a 9-5, can't take criticism, wants part-time).
15. **Final CTA band**: "Think you're a fit? Apply today." + 2 short motivating statements + Apply button.
16. Footer.

Notably absent on the reference: salary, an on-page form (Apply Now goes off-page),
hiring process steps, FAQ.
